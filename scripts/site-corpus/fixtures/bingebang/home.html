BingeBang - Watch Free Movies & TV Shows Online

BingeBang is a free streaming site for movies and TV shows. Press play on thousands of films and complete series in HD, many of them in 4K, with no ads and no sign-up needed. The catalogue updates around the clock, so new releases and fresh episodes appear as soon as they can be played.

Browse movies and TV shows, see what is trending, check the TV calendar for this week's new episodes, or filter the whole library by genre, year and streaming service in Explore.

What you can do on BingeBang

Stream in HD and 4K

Every title plays right in your browser. Pick a quality yourself or let the player follow your connection.

Switch servers in one tap

Most titles are on several servers. If one does not play, pick another from the player's settings menu.

Subtitles in your language

Use the built-in subtitles, search OpenSubtitles in more than 40 languages, or upload your own file.

Download to watch later

Movie and episode pages list direct download links with their quality and file size.

Your own lists

A free account adds a watchlist, favourites, resume across devices, new-episode alerts, recommendations and achievements.

Any screen

Works on phones, tablets, laptops and smart-TV browsers, with Chromecast and picture-in-picture where your device allows it.

Is BingeBang free?

Yes. Watching on BingeBang is free, there are no ads and you do not need an account. Signing up is optional and only adds personal features such as your watchlist and resume across devices.

The official BingeBang website

The official BingeBang address is bingebang.sx. Our domain directory at bingebang.club or bingebang.domains always lists every working BingeBang domain, and any change of address is announced first on our Discord server. Sites that use the BingeBang name but are not listed there are copycats - do not use them, and never enter your BingeBang password on them.
